How calls are graded
  • A call is graded once its window closes: entry is the first close on/after the call date, exit the last close inside the window, and SPY is graded over the same days. The window follows the run's outlook: the next session for day trades, five trading days for short term, about a month for long term.
  • BUY and SELL are right or wrong by alpha vs SPY beyond ±1%; inside that band they are neutral. HOLD is right when the stock slides more than 2% (standing aside saved money), wrong when it gains more than 2% (a missed gain), neutral in between.
  • Hit rate counts decisive calls only; neutrals stay visible as their own count. A repeated analysis of the same call on the same day counts once, so re-runs cannot multiply one outcome.
